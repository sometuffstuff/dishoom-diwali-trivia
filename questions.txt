# DISHOOM — DIWALI RAPID TRIVIA
# Format: Question | Correct answer
# Questions are presented in the order listed.

What title is given to the festival of Diwali? | Festival of lights
What are the small oil lamps lit during Diwali called? | Diyas (or small oil lamps)
Which goddess is celebrated during Diwali? | Lakshmi
Which festival features the famous Garba dance? | Navratri
Which sweet is traditionally offered during Ganesh Chaturthi? | Modak
Which Indian city is famous for its Blue City nickname? | Jodhpur
Which Indian festival features decorated elephants in Kerala? | Thrissur Pooram
What is the traditional Indian hanging decoration made of mango leaves called? | Toran
Who composed the Ramcharitmanas? | Tulsidas
What colour of clothing is traditionally worn on Vasant Panchami? | Yellow
What is the name of Lakshmi's sister, who is associated with misfortune? | Alakshmi
Which festival commemorates the churning of the cosmic ocean (Samudra Manthan) through ritual bathing in sacred rivers every 12 years? | Kumbh Mela
What is the name of the traditional boat race associated with Onam? | Vallam Kali
What is the name of the traditional harvest festival of Assam? | Bhogali
What is the name of the mountain lifted by Krishna? | Govardhan
What is the name of the sacred thread tied during Raksha Bandhan in Rajasthan? | Lumba
Which ancient kingdom did Lord Rama rule? | Kosala
In South India, what is the day preceding the main Diwali celebration, associated with the defeat of Narakasura, called? | Narak Chaturdashi
What is the name of the intricate floor art traditionally made using coloured powders, rice flour, or flower petals during Diwali? | Rangoli
Which historic city in Uttar Pradesh organises a massive Deepotsav prior to Diwali along the Saryu River? | Ayodhya
Which Jain Tirthankara attained Nirvana on Diwali? | Mahavira
Which city is traditionally associated with Krishna's defeat of Narakasura? | Pragjyotisha
Whose release or liberation do Sikhs commemorate on Diwali as Bandi Chhor Divas? | Guru Hargobind Sahib
What is the traditional Marathi name for the day after Diwali that marks the victory of King Bali? | Bali Pratipada (or Bali Padyami)
The day Ganesha began writing the Mahabharata or Krishna gave the Akshaya Patra to the Pandavas is known as what? | Akshaya Tritiya
What is the traditional name for gambling on Diwali night? | Dyuta
Which legendary warrior in the Mahabharata chose to wait on a bed of arrows for the Makar Sankranti period before passing away? | Bhishma Pitamah
